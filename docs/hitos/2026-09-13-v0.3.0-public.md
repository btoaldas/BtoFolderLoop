## Remaining limits and recovery

The application is ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. This installation does not establish warning-free first launch on another Mac or broad OS/cloud-provider compatibility. The public package supports Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or newer; Intel requires a separately tested source build. Filesystem race/provider limitations remain documented in SECURITY.md.

The previous bundle and preinstallation/migration database backups provide reviewable recovery material, not permission to overwrite newer history. Any application/database rollback must preserve subsequent work and verify schema compatibility. Future releases must publish their archive before advertising the signed feed and must never weaken verification as a fallback.
